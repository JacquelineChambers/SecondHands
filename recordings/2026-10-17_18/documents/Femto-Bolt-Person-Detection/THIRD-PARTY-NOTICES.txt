detector.py adapts the upstream YOLOv5s preprocessing and detection pipeline.
Changes include person-only filtering, objectness-times-class confidence,
vectorized postprocessing, factory calibration, and measured XYZ extraction.
The upstream repository is retained unchanged for comparison.

The included YOLOv5s ONNX weights come from the upstream example's models
directory, corresponding to Ultralytics YOLOv5 v7.0. Ultralytics model/source
licensing applies independently of the Orbbec example's Apache license.
https://github.com/ultralytics/yolov5/tree/v7.0

The separately installed Orbbec SDK and proprietary depth-engine DLL retain
their vendor license terms. OpenCV, ONNX Runtime, NumPy and Pillow retain
their respective licenses in the isolated Python environment.
