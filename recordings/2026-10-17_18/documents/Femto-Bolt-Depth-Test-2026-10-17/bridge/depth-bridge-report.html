Why the original SDK failed

The SDK searched for the 1024 × 512 calibration mode at 5 FPS. The standard Media Foundation and DirectShow routes exposed only a 30 FPS entry. Direct KS proxy access exposed all five underlying formats and allowed calibration and phase capture. The original error was therefore consistent with capture API format enumeration, rather than missing calibration data.

This verifies working sensor capture and depth decoding. Absolute distance accuracy has not been checked against a ruler or surveyed target. Valid pixel coverage depends on the scene, range, surfaces, and lighting; 100% coverage is not expected for every view. This prototype provides a local viewer and exports, not a virtual camera or a drop-in replacement for every Orbbec application.
